Korean Country

Korean Dress

Korean clothes are beautiful and unique. The traditional Korean dress is called Hanbok. It is usually worn during festivals, weddings, and special occasions. Hanbok comes in many beautiful colours and designs. Modern Korean fashion is also very popular around the world because of K-pop, Korean dramas, and Korean celebrities. Korean people wear different styles of clothes, such as jeans, shirts, dresses, and jackets. Korean fashion is famous for its simple, stylish, and attractive designs.

Korean Foods

Korean food is delicious and popular around the world. It has many different flavours and colourful dishes. Kimchi is one of the most famous Korean foods. It is made from fermented vegetables, usually cabbage. Other popular Korean foods include bibimbap, bulgogi, kimbap, and tteokbokki. Korean people often eat rice, vegetables, meat, and soup. Some Korean foods are spicy, while others are sweet or mild. Korean food has become very popular because of Korean dramas and K-pop. I would love to try different Korean dishes.
